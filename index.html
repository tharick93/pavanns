FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DomainVerse and how does it work? +
DomainVerse is a social domain marketplace where you can register, buy, sell, and trade domain names. Create your account, search for domains, register instantly, and connect with our community of 850,000+ domain enthusiasts and investors worldwide.
How long does domain registration take? +
Domain registration is instant! After completing checkout (which takes about 60 seconds), your domain is registered and active within minutes. DNS propagation typically takes 24-48 hours globally, though most changes resolve within 1-4 hours.
Can I transfer my existing domains to DomainVerse? +
Absolutely! We support domain transfers from all major registrars including GoDaddy, Namecheap, and Google Domains. The transfer process is free and typically completes within 5-7 days. Your existing registration period is preserved during transfer.
How does the domain marketplace work? +
Our marketplace lets you list domains for sale with a Buy Now price or accept offers. When a sale is agreed, our secure escrow system holds funds until the domain is transferred. We charge a 5% success fee on completed sales with no upfront listing costs.
Is my payment information secure? +
Yes, completely. We use 256-bit SSL encryption, are PCI-DSS compliant, and never store card data on our servers. Payments are processed through Stripe, one of the world's most trusted payment processors. We also support PayPal, crypto, and bank transfers.
What TLDs and extensions do you support? +
We support 500+ domain extensions including all major gTLDs (.com, .net, .org), popular new TLDs (.io, .ai, .app, .dev, .co), country codes, and specialty extensions. If you need a specific TLD not listed, contact our support team.
